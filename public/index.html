          Welcome to MoleBridge

          What this is

          A stoichiometry problem, worked one step at a time. You enter every value along the way — the balanced coefficients, the molar mass, the moles, the ratio — and you cannot move on until the step in front of you is right.

          When a step is wrong, MoleBridge works out which mistake produces that exact number and tells you, instead of just marking it wrong. If the mistake is an algebra one, it shows you three lines of the algebra using your own numbers.

          What this is not

          • It is not a solver. It will not do a problem for you, and it never shows you an answer before you have tried it.
          • It is not a grade. The code at the end records how far you got and where you got stuck. Your teacher decides what that is worth.
          • It does not know who you are. It asks for the roster number your teacher gave you and nothing else — no name, no email, no account.

          How a session goes

          1. Type in the roster number and assignment key your teacher put on the board.
          2. Work each problem stage by stage. Getting one wrong costs you nothing except that the problem stops counting as first-try.
          3. At the end you get a short code. Type it into the Canvas assignment.

          Nothing is stored and nothing is sent

          MoleBridge has no account and no server. Nothing you type leaves the device, and nothing is kept after you close the tab — so if you stop halfway, you start again. That is the trade for it working with no connection and holding nothing about you.

          Periodic table

          Pick an element to see its name and weight written out.

          These are atomic weights, for one atom's worth. A molar mass is these added up for everything in the formula — which is the part you are practising, so this will not do it for you.

          Calculator

          This does numbers only. It will not work out a molar mass or convert a unit for you — those are the parts you are practising, and MoleBridge can only tell you what went wrong in them if you do them.
